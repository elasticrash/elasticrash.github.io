One of my usual habits when I walk around the city is to have my headphones on and listen to either music or podcast interviews. Yesterday, for some reason, I decided to listen to [Ezra Klein interviewing Bill Gates](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=A_156w0aYtU). As weird as it may seem, Bill Gates was someone I never really respected, but I was still curious about what he had to say.

The thing that struck me by surprise was that I was listening to both brilliant and senile opinions from a single person. A conflicted old man who clearly hasn't thought things through. There was a particular moment in the interview where Ezra Klein was trying to communicate a pretty clear point. What is the point of investing millions in AI learning languages that only very few people speak if your main opinion is that everyone will be unemployed or dead? To which Gates replied that those people have never met a doctor in their lives and that AI could save their lives. Oh yes, and it will also advise them on how to do their jobs better. Yes!! The jobs that they will no longer have. I would understand this part if he was more optimistic about the future of humanity.

This brings us to the second and more surprising part was when Ezra Klein was trying to explain that if the world's AI systems are restricted to the US and China, and if American businesses contribute to a pension-like fund for US citizens to have a basic income, what will this mean for other countries, with the US having complete dominance over them? And he just brushed it off. I think deep down he believes that AI will unite every nation in the world, which I very much doubt.

If I had to sum up Bill Gates's opinions, it would be to just hand over everything to AI after building the "control layer," and live a happy, ignorant life while AI governs us. But ultimately, this needs to be "the people's choice" whether we keep or ban AI. But at the same time, he stated that protesting against AI and data centers is pointless, and those will be build regardless of any efforts to oppose them. 

He argued that AI currently has the capability to bring down governments and start biological warfare, and that we need to act and try to stop it before an incident happens. But if the current generation of AI already has that capability freely available, how long do we need to wait before something like that happens? Surely bad actors won't wait if they basically have to do nothing themselves. At the same time, we already have someone planning [a mass shooting with AI in Canada](https://www.theguardian.com/technology/2026/sep/22/british-columbia-sues-openai-sam-altman-tumbler-ridge-school-shooting).

But since we are talking about extreme scenarios. What will happen, if at some point we get such a massive [CME event](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Coronal_mass_ejection), that will destroy a large portion of the planet's electronics? If something like that happen today, projection state that a recovery will take 2-5 years with a big impact in the worldwide population. But if we over-rely on AI for everything. Would we even have the knowledge to rebuild everything from scratch?
